# LIFELOG Privacy Policy (draft — owner must review and host before Play publication)

LIFELOG processes device-usage statistics that Android provides after you grant Usage Access: when apps move to the
foreground/background, when the screen becomes interactive, lock/unlock and startup/shutdown moments, Android's own
usage summaries, and battery-level samples.

- **Storage:** only on your device, in LIFELOG's private storage. Excluded from Android cloud backup.
- **Sharing:** none. LIFELOG has no internet permission and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
- **Not collected:** message or notification contents, passwords, keystrokes, touches, screen contents, location, contacts, calls.
- **Your control:** pause tracking, revoke Usage Access, export (optionally password-encrypted), restore, or delete all data in the app.
- **Purchase date:** only if you enter it; it is stored as user-provided and never verified.
- **Contact:** <owner to add>
